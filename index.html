07 FAQ

Common questions.

What are your opening hours?

We're open 24/7, 365 days a year. Your fitness journey shouldn't have time constraints. Train whenever you want.

Do I need to be fit to join?

Absolutely not! KARNA welcomes all fitness levels. Our trainers will create a program tailored to your current level and help you progress at your own pace.

What equipment do you have?

We have over 200 pieces of equipment including Olympic dumbbells, barbells, cable machines, kettlebells, treadmills, rowing machines, Smith machines, and a dedicated functional training area.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! We offer a free trial session. Come experience KARNA for yourself — no commitment required. Just show up and we'll take care of the rest.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with no hidden fees. We believe in earning your membership every single day through the quality of our service.
